Explore a 3D voxel meadow, jump between hills, break blocks and build your own structures. This is the first foundation of our larger browser adventure.

Controls
WASD: move · Mouse: look · Space: jump · Left click: break block · Right click: place block · 1–8: select block · Esc: release mouse.
On phones/tablets use the on-screen movement and action buttons. Click the game to capture your mouse.
